## What you get

Monthly:

1. **One written intelligence brief per month** (12 per year, 2,000–4,000 words each) — three sections per brief:
   - **What landed in the public record this month** — every CRE-AI regulatory matter, settlement, consent order, enforcement action, or significant guidance document, cited verbatim, with primary-source links
   - **What the framework would have caught** — for each landed matter, which patterns engage and what the audit-evidence bundle would have surfaced

Cumulative:

2. **Private failure-mode catalog access** — gated repository of operator-side failure-mode signals not in the public `FAILURE-MODES.md`. Each entry is a synthesized lesson from confidential engagements (de-identified) or from non-public regulator-behavior patterns.
3. **Deposition-shaped playbook library** — playbooks for specific operator-side audit scenarios likely to arise in regulatory inquiry, deposition, or BigLaw discovery. Authored playbooks; gated.
4. **Named-matter risk briefings on demand** — when a major matter lands or a major regulatory change publishes, a dedicated brief within 5 business days, mapped to the subscriber's declared exposure surface.

## What's NOT in the public framework

- **Operator-side failure-mode catalog beyond the public matrix.** The public `FAILURE-MODES.md` documents 8 classes. The private catalog documents 30+ failure-mode signals accumulated from confidential engagements.
- **Deposition-shaped playbooks.** The public framework is methodology; the playbooks are operationalized response patterns for specific scenarios (CFPB letter received; FTC inquiry letter received; class-action complaint filed; Big-4 audit qualification proposed; etc.).
- **Monthly cadence and named-matter responsiveness.** The public framework releases on its own cadence; the subscription guarantees monthly publication and on-demand named-matter briefs.
- **Confidential engagement-derived intelligence.** Each subscriber engagement feeds back into the framework's understanding of operator-side risk patterns; subscribers see this synthesis (de-identified) before it surfaces in public artifacts (if it ever does).

## What's NOT in scope

- 1:1 confidential consulting on subscriber-specific exposure (that's the Diagnostic, Audit, or Retainer)
- Legal opinion or counsel
- Direct access to other subscribers (that's the Practitioner Bench)
- Code implementation or framework deployment in subscriber stacks
- Recording or redistribution of subscription content beyond the subscriber's firm
