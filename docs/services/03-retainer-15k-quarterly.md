# Retainer — $15K/quarter

**Duration:** Recurring quarterly engagement (minimum 4 quarters)

**Deliverable:** Quarterly audit-evidence rerun on the operator's current AI surface + new-incident coverage brief + regulatory-update brief, each quarter. Cumulative coverage compounds over the year; year-over-year comparisons available from quarter five onward.

**Target buyer:** Repeat audit clients (typically post-Audit operators continuing the relationship) · Big-4 advisory practices retaining methodology access · operators with continuous AI-deployment cadence requiring ongoing audit-evidence

## What you get

Per quarter:

1. **Quarterly audit-evidence rerun** — the harness re-applied to the operator's current AI surface; new audit-evidence bundle delivered
2. **New-incident coverage brief** — every CRE-AI matter that landed in the public record during the quarter, mapped to the operator's exposure surface (typically 0–3 matters per quarter)
3. **Regulatory-update brief** — every operative regulatory change during the quarter (CFPB, FTC, HUD, state AI bills, NIST AI RMF profile updates) mapped to the operator's compliance surface
4. **30-minute quarterly readout** — live conversation with the operator's CTO / CAIO / risk function
5. **Confidential client memo** filed in the private engagement corpus

Cumulative over a year:

6. **Year-end summary** — 4-quarter trend analysis; year-over-year comparison from year two
7. **Engagement-renewal recommendation** — explicit recommendation on whether to renew, expand (to the Workshop or Cohort tier), or transition to a different engagement shape

## Methodology

- Anchored on the most-recent published `cre-agent-audit` release (DOI updated per release)
- Each quarter's audit-evidence rerun uses the same harness as the $40K Audit, applied to the operator's evolving surface
- New-incident coverage brief is grounded in the public Regulatory-Incident Replay framework — each new matter the framework adds to `examples/regulatory-incidents/` is integrated into the operator's quarterly brief
- Regulatory-update brief uses primary-source citation discipline (no paraphrasing)

## What's NOT in the public framework

- **Quarterly continuity.** The public framework releases on its own cadence; this engagement guarantees quarterly delivery on the operator's cadence.
- **Operator-specific regulatory mapping.** The public framework names regulatory anchors generically; this brief maps each new regulatory change to the operator's specific exposure surface.
- **Year-over-year trend visibility.** Audit-evidence accumulates engagement-over-engagement; year-over-year comparison is something only the retained engagement produces.
- **Private corpus dividend.** Lessons from other (confidential) engagements that inform framework v.next reach the Retainer client first — through the quarterly readout, before the framework's public roadmap publishes.

## What's NOT in scope

- New AI-surface scope additions beyond the engagement-letter baseline (priced at $5K per added surface per quarter or rolled into a renewal-cycle Audit at $40K)
- Code implementation
- Legal opinion or counsel
- Engagements outside the operator's named scope (other portcos, other operators in the same fund)
- Speaking engagements or external-facing co-marketing (those are Workshop scope)
