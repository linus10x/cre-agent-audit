# Cohort — $50K–$200K

**Duration:** 8-week structured program (20–40 seats per cohort, 2 cohorts per year)

**Deliverable:** A peer-learning cohort taught live by Kunjar Bhaduri. Each cohort member completes the operator-side AI governance curriculum, contributes one new named matter to the public `examples/regulatory-incidents/` directory as their capstone, and joins the alumni network. Each cohort produces eight matter PRs and 20–40 senior practitioners trained on the methodology.

**Target buyer:** Senior practicing CTOs / CAIOs at top-50 multifamily / Big-4 AI assurance practice senior managers / BigLaw partners / PE operating partners with AI-governance mandate / executive-education programs at Wharton, Booth, Sloan, INSEAD

## What you get

1. **8 weekly 2-hour live sessions** (16 hours total) — Kunjar Bhaduri facilitating each session
2. **Pre-program intake call** — 30 minutes per participant to scope their capstone matter
3. **Weekly assigned reading + exercises** — the Regulatory-Incident Replay framework, the seven services portfolio reading, three matter walkthroughs, and a primary-source-citation discipline exercise
4. **Capstone project** — each participant contributes one new named-matter replay to the public `examples/regulatory-incidents/` directory as a PR; matters are reviewed and merged (or rejected with feedback) by Kunjar before cohort close
5. **Cohort-end private alumni Slack/Discord** — 12-month access; renewed annually through Practitioner Bench enrollment
6. **Joint case-study publication opportunity** — selected capstone matters are co-published with credited cohort attribution
7. **Certificate of completion** — naming the cohort cycle, the capstone matter contributed, and the participant's framework engagement (no accreditation implied)
8. **Confidential client memo per cohort member** filed in the private engagement corpus

## Methodology

- Anchored on `cre-agent-audit` v0.2.1+ (DOI [10.5281/zenodo.20434575](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.20434575))
- The Regulatory-Incident Replay framework is the textbook
- Sessions sequence: (1) Operator-side category claim · (2) Pattern walkthrough · (3) TransUnion matter live · (4) SafeRent matter live · (5) RealPage-as-alleged with the disclaimer discipline · (6) Audit-evidence bundle production · (7) Capstone-matter peer review · (8) Cohort close + alumni handoff
- Each cohort member's capstone matter is a contributable PR; the cohort produces public artifact mass each cycle

## What's NOT in the public framework

- **Structured peer-learning environment.** The public framework is documentation; the cohort is structured learning with feedback loops, peer review, and accountability cadence.
- **Capstone publication path.** Each cohort member produces a public artifact (a new matter replay) — a credentialing artifact that compounds the cohort member's own reputation, not just Kunjar's.
- **Private alumni network.** The 12-month alumni Slack/Discord with practitioner peers is gated; the public framework cannot offer it.
- **Direct multi-week access to the author.** 16 hours of live facilitated time across 8 weeks. The published framework is text; this is conversation.
- **Confidential corpus exposure (de-identified).** Cohort sessions include lessons from confidential engagements that don't appear in the public docs — surfaced as anonymous patterns and cohort discussion material.

## What's NOT in scope

- Code implementation in participants' stacks beyond the in-cohort exercises and the capstone PR
- Legal opinion or counsel
- Per-participant 1:1 confidential consulting (that's the Diagnostic or Audit)
- Recording or distribution rights for the cohort sessions beyond participants
- Continuing-education credit (no accreditation program exists yet)
